# Presets

Ready-to-import GtrBoard presets. They are plain JSON (`.json` or
`.gtrpreset`) and can be imported with **⌘O** or **Browse Presets → Import**.

| File | Tone |
|---|---|
| [`Enter Sandman GPT.json`](Enter%20Sandman%20GPT.json) | Metallica *Black Album* rhythm: tight TS-style boost, Mesa-style high gain, closed 4×12, scooped EQ, small room. Bridge humbucker, standard E. |
| [`Linkin Park - Hybrid Theory.json`](Linkin%20Park%20-%20Hybrid%20Theory.json) | *Hybrid Theory / Meteora* rhythm: drop D, tight high gain, more upper-mid bite than Metallica, completely dry. Good for *One Step Closer*, *Papercut*. |
| [`Linkin Park - Faint.json`](Linkin%20Park%20-%20Faint.json) | The aggressive end: faster gate, more gain, brighter presence, tight low cut. Good for *Faint* and *Given Up*. |

> These are original DSP approximations, not amp captures or exact record
> reproductions. They are loudness-calibrated to the factory presets; adjust
> the **PRESET** slider in the footer to taste.
