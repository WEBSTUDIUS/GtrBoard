# Presets

Ready-to-import GtrBoard presets. They are plain JSON (`.json` or
`.gtrpreset`) and can be imported with **⌘O** or **Browse Presets → Import**.

| File | Author | Tone |
|---|---|---|
| [`Enter Sandman GPT.json`](Enter%20Sandman%20GPT.json) | AI + GPT-6 | High-gain Metallica-inspired rhythm: gate, tight overdrive boost, high-gain amp, closed 4×12, scooped graphic EQ. No reverb. |

> These files are community/AI examples, not factory presets. Factory presets
> are built into the app. Adjust the **PRESET** level in the footer after
> importing to match your guitar and interface.
